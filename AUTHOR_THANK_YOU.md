# Thank you to the author

Thank you to Moeen Nehzati for making *Universal Representation of Generalized
Convex Functions and their Gradients* available. The combination of
generalized convex analysis, optimal transport, and auction applications made
it possible to organize the audit around proof certificates, exact
counterexamples, and continuous objectives.

This repository is an independent reproduction audit. It is not an official
implementation and does not imply author review or endorsement.
