Section V globally assumes compact Euclidean `X` and `Y` and locally Lipschitz
`Phi`. Section V.C defines a finite parameter as lean when every support is
active somewhere, then Theorem 4 states that this lean subset is convex.
The claim is universal over fixed finite supports under those stated global
assumptions.

The exact counterexample uses finite compact `X={0,1}` and `Y={0,1,2}` and a
finite-valued surplus, hence all suprema are attained and local Lipschitzness
has a finite bound. It does not assert failure under extra unstated conditions
such as continuum convex domains or a bilinear surplus.
